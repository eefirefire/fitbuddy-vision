Abstract
FitBuddy AI analyzes squat videos and delivers professional-grade feedback instantly: rep count, a 10-point form score, and corrective cues. Using Google Gemini's multimodal reasoning, five prompt strategies were benchmarked over 5 trials each and scored by an LLM judge against human-annotated ground truth. The Self-Reflection Pass scored highest (7.40/10), outperforming Zero-Shot (4.46), Three-Shot (5.81), and CoT-Verification alone (5.70). The production system combines Self-Reflection with CoT for maximum accuracy. Only 15 JPEG frames reach the Gemini API — raw video is never uploaded to any server.
Introduction & Motivation
Filming workouts is common — interpreting the footage is not. Without a coach, people cannot verify squat depth, knee tracking, or bracing, risking injury and stalled progress.
No feedback loop: recorded footage is useless without expert eyes
Access gap: personal trainers are expensive and unavailable
Privacy: existing tools require uploading full video to third-party servers
FitBuddy AI solves all three: instant rep count, form score, and corrective cues from a short video clip.
Materials & Methods
1
Data collection: Reddit r/formcheck + YouTube coaching breakdowns — videos paired with expert critique.
2
Ground truth: 40 videos standardized into a 4-file JSON hierarchy: description / structured_feedback / evaluation.
3
Benchmark: 5 prompt strategies × 5 trials, scored by Gemini 2.5 Flash (LLM judge) against human annotations.
4
Deployment: Best strategies combined. 15 frames via Canvas API → Gemini → JSON validation → dashboard.
Key Learnings
💡
Reflection is the highest-leverage interventionSelf-verification alone (7.40) outperformed Three-Shot (5.81) and CoT standalone (5.70).
📊
Measure before decidingWithout the evaluation harness, Three-Shot "felt" more robust — the judge ranked it 3rd.
🔒
Client-side extraction removes infrastructure15 JPEG frames only. Raw video never leaves the browser — no server costs, no privacy risk.
✅
Precision over recall for coachingThe reflection pass reduces false positives — the right tradeoff for a coaching tool.

Discussion & Conclusion
What this shows
A self-verification pass — no extra training — raised alignment from 4.46 to 7.40/10 against human expert ground truth. The most impactful single change.
The pattern generalizes
Sample frames in-browser → one verified exemplar → model self-verifies → enforce JSON schema. Applies to any video-based movement analysis task.
Broader impact
High-quality AI coaching without expensive infrastructure — expert-level feedback accessible to anyone with a smartphone and a short video.
